# TasteBuds Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 16, 2026**

TasteBuds ("the app," "we," "us") helps you and your friends decide where to eat together. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. We built TasteBuds to be simple, and this policy follows suit.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address, and a unique account identifier from the sign-in provider. If you sign in with Apple, you may choose to hide your email address, in which case we receive Apple's private relay address instead. You may also create a nickname and choose an avatar and short bio within the app.

**Location.** With your permission, the app uses your device's location to find restaurants near you. Your location is sent to Google's Places and Geocoding services to perform searches. We do not store a history of your locations. You can also type a location manually instead of using device location, and you can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

**Content you create.** This includes places you save, restaurant reviews you write, star ratings, comments and likes on reviews, your friend connections and friend requests, and the swipe sessions you participate in (including which restaurant you and a partner matched on).

**Information we do not collect.** TasteBuds does not show ads, does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not sell or share your personal information with data brokers. We do not collect contacts, photos, health data, or precise location history.

## How We Use Information

We use your information solely to operate the app: to sign you in, find restaurants near you, run swipe sessions between you and your friends, display your saved places and match history, and show your reviews to your connected friends ("TasteBuds"). We do not use your information for advertising or marketing.

## How Information Is Shared

**With other users.** Your name, nickname, and avatar are visible to users you connect with. Your reviews, review comments, and likes are intended to be visible to your connected friends. When you complete a session with a partner, each of you can see the match and the other's name.

**With service providers.** TasteBuds is built on Google Firebase (authentication and database, operated by Google LLC) and uses Google Maps Platform (restaurant search and geocoding) and Apple's Sign in with Apple. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy commitments:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

**Legal requirements.** We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users.

We do not sell personal information.

## Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Access is protected by authentication and server-side security rules so that, for example, only you can modify your profile and saved places, and only session participants can modify a session. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your account data and content remain stored while your account is active. You can delete individual reviews within the app. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below and we will delete your data within 30 days.

## Children

TasteBuds is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your Choices

You can revoke location permission in your device settings (search will require a typed location instead). You can decline friend requests, and you control what reviews and content you post. You may request account deletion at any time.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
